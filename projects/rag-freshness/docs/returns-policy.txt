Customers can return any item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Items must be returned in their original packaging with the receipt included.
Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the item.
Items marked final sale cannot be returned or exchanged.
To start a return, sign in to your account and choose the order you want to send back.
Return shipping is free for members, and guests pay a flat 6 dollar fee.
